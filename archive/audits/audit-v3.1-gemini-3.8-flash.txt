### 1. Biological Reality: The Inevitable Neuro-Endocrine Crash

#### The Failure Modes
* **The Math is a Fiction:** 22:00 in bed to 04:45 wake is theoretically 6h45m. For an unmedicated ADHD brain with delayed phase sleep syndrome (DPSS) and high evening rumination, sleep latency averages 45–75 minutes. True sleep is 5h30m–5h45m. Day 1 logged 3h21m. Melatonin shifts phase by ~30–45 minutes at best; it does not eliminate cortisol spikes from evening blue light or overtraining.
* **Cortisol / Overtraining Syndrome (OTS) by Day 10–14:** Muay Thai camp (30-min run + 60-min intense striking/clinch) with near-zero glycogen + caloric deficit elevates systemic cortisol. High cortisol impairs prefrontal cortex (PFC) executive function, obliterates working memory needed for DSA, and drastically lowers impulse control.
* **The Crash Point:** By Week 2, nervous system fatigue will present as physical leadenness, brain fog, and an uncontrollable biological drive to binge on high-dopamine inputs (reels, sugar, sexual release).

#### Concrete Fixes
1. **Move Wake Window to 05:45 AM for Camp Days; Anchor Fajr at the Tail:** 
   In Thane in September, Fajr adhan is ~05:08 AM and sunrise is ~06:22 AM. Hanafi madhhab explicitly prefers *Isfar* (delaying Fajr until the horizon is well lit, roughly 30–40 min before sunrise, ~05:40 AM). Sleeping 22:00 to 05:40 gives a solid 7+ hours of physiological recovery. Drop Tahajjud to Saturday/Sunday mornings only during September.
2. **Post-Camp Glycogen Refeed (Non-Ketogenic Carbs):** 
   Add 40–50g of clean, fast-acting complex carbohydrates (e.g., 200g boiled sweet potato or 1 cup white rice) specifically in Meal 2 immediately post-workout. This blunts post-exercise cortisol, lowers systemic adrenaline, and protects cognitive glucose availability for office LeetCode.

* **The "Zero-Carb Dinner" Lie:** Carbs are precursors to serotonin, which converts to melatonin. Zero-carb dinners with elevated evening cortisol almost universally cause insomnia, night awakenings, and racing thoughts in ADHD individuals—directly driving the 2 AM relapse cycle.

#### Concrete Fixes
1. **Abandon "Near-Keto"; Run a Targeted Glycemic Deficit (120–150g Carbs):** 
   Eat 2050 kcal with 160g protein, 60g fat, and 140g clean carbs. 
   * Morning pre-workout: 2 dates.
   * Post-camp breakfast: 1 cup cooked oats or white rice alongside eggs/whey.
   * Dinner: Add 1 boiled potato or 1 roti (~20-30g carbs) with sabzi/meat to facilitate tryptophan crossing the blood-brain barrier for natural melatonin production.
2. **Drop the Daily Ultra 7% Milk:** 
   Switching entirely to toned milk (3%) saves ~150 kcal of saturated fat that can be redeployed as complex carbohydrates without altering total calories or protein floor.
