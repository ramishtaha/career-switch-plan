## 1. TAHAJJUD — it fits. Here's the arithmetic.

Maghrib 19:09 → Fajr 05:20 = **10h11 of night. Thirds are 3h24, so the last third opens ~01:56.** A 04:50 wake sits deep inside it.

**Fiqh order:** Witr is the **last** prayer of the night, so it goes **after** Tahajjud, not after Isha ([SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/praying-witr-after-tahajjud-make-ups/), [IslamQA](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/216236)).

## 2. DAY TYPES — three labels, never a decision

**Run days are fixed by weekday label. Never chosen, only read:**

- **MON / WED / SAT → run days.** Run + (Mon/Wed) strength.
- **TUE / THU → DSA mornings.** No Cult. Mornings transferred to DSA.
- **FRI → Cult day.** This is the 4th session that holds the maintenance floor.

**MMA maintenance floor: 4 sessions/week — Mon, Wed, Sat, Fri. Below 4 is drift, not rest. Never zero.**

**Cues (the cue isn't the clock, so two Cult times cost nothing):** habit strength holds when activity + context repeat, and routine cues beat clock cues ([cue study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11201238/), [RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33405284/)).
- **Tue/Thu DSA cue:** *adhkar done → SEED → laptop opens 06:00.*
- **Mon/Wed/Fri DSA cue:** *shower ends → laptop opens.*
- **Gym cue:** *adhkar done → kit on → out.*

**Run conditions (load management, not nagging):**
1. **20 min easy, conversational. Never intervals, never longer.** Short easy fasted runs are the one fasted session the evidence supports; harder fasted work degrades performance ([review](https://sportcoaching.com.au/the-real-truth-about-fasting-running/), [meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12195750/)).
2. **Not truly fasted: dates + water before you go out.** Chronic low-carb-availability training tracks with bone injury ([study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11369321/)). Two dates removes that.
3. **Run-day Cult = strength/mobility, capped at 45 min. Never HIIT.** Run days are ~65 min of training on 7h sleep; ≤7h carries ~1.7× injury risk ([ACSM](https://journals.lww.com/acsm-csmr/fulltext/2021/06000/sleep_and_injury_risk.3.aspx)). Don't stack two hard sessions.

**Run-day DSA: 08:25–09:15, 50 min. New problems ARE allowed.** The re-solve work moved to the evening slot, where spaced retrieval earns its keep at g ≈ 0.74 ([meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8)). **Not at the desk by 08:35 → SEED-only, box still ticks.**

## 6. SLEEP ARITHMETIC — honest math

| | Old | **FINAL** |
|---|---|---|
| Asleep / wake | ~23:45 → 04:30 | **21:45 → 04:50** |
| **Night, consolidated** | ~4h45 | **7h05** |
| Post-Fajr sleep | 1h | **0** |
| Post-gym nap | 1h | **0 — the block occupies the slot** |
| Shape | ~6h45, **3 pieces** | **7h05, one piece** |

**04:50, not 05:00.** Those 10 minutes buy Tahajjud, and 7h05 still clears the ≤7h line where injury risk climbs ([ACSM](https://journals.lww.com/acsm-csmr/fulltext/2021/06000/sleep_and_injury_risk.3.aspx)). At 05:00: no Tahajjud. At 04:30: under the line on run days. **04:50 is the only number where both the sunnah and the training load survive.**

Fragmented sleep degrades processing even when hours look fine ([study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2851141/)). **Weekday nap: none, run days included. Weekend nap: 20–30 min, timer, inside Qailulah.** 20 min = benefit without inertia; 1h+ = slow-wave grogginess ([20 vs 30](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16796222/)). Dragging by W2 → lights out 21:15. **Never a morning nap.**

## 10. MISS PROTOCOL — blank rows are the enemy, not misses

- **Miss once → nothing happens. Log the row anyway.** A single lapse doesn't materially affect habit formation ([Lally](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/32898894_How_are_habits_formed_Modeling_habit_formation_in_the_real_world)).
- **Miss twice → the next day auto-drops to the Low Bar.** No deciding. Low Bar = a hit.
- **Never two blanks in a row.** A miss you logged is data. A blank row is the failure.
- **Re-planning after a miss is banned.** See CLOSURE.
- **Guilt rule:** write *"That was a missed rep, not a failed plan."* Then stop ([study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-018-9926-3)).
- **Tracker:** one paper card, 28 boxes. `✓` = full block · `S` = SEED only · `M` = logged miss · blank = the enemy. No app.

**WITNESS = HERMES. There is no human in this loop, and that's fine.**
- Every evening, one line to the Telegram close-out: **"done"** or **"missed."**
- **Report streak is tracked separately from day streak.** You can miss the day and still keep the report streak. **Reporting a miss is a win.**
