## 4. The B12 pattern: why "more supplement" or "more beef" is the wrong fix

- **The pattern:** deficient (181) **while eating** beef, eggs and dairy daily → raised (to 659, presumably by a supplement or injection) → **falling steadily** after it stopped, still on the same B12-rich diet.
- **Why it matters:** food B12 needs stomach acid plus intrinsic factor to be absorbed. High-dose oral supplements partly bypass this (roughly 1% absorbs passively), and so do injections. So a supplement *can* push the number up while the underlying problem stays. That problem is likely one of: *H. pylori*, autoimmune (pernicious) gastritis, PPI or antacid use, or metformin (unlikely at his age). The level then drifts back down whenever the supplement stops. **The fall on a good diet is the diagnostic clue.**
- **Why it matters for the arc:** low B12 shows up as fatigue, low mood and poor concentration, which is exactly what an ADHD brain can't spare during a 3 h study block.
- **🩺 Ask the GP for:** MMA + homocysteine (tissue B12), *H. pylori*, intrinsic-factor / parietal-cell antibodies, a written list of past B12 doses. **Then** the doctor picks the maintenance route: high-dose oral vs injections. Don't self-dose to "fix the number" before these tests, because a supplement can mask the result.
- **Vitamin D:** no action beyond maintenance if he's already taking it (🩺 dose). Free source: 10–15 min of morning sun on bare forearms during the **09:00 read block** (read on the balcony).

## 5. Iron: food-first and timing (🩺 ferritin before any tablet)

- Beef is heme iron, the best-absorbed kind. **Intake isn't the issue. Absorption timing and training load are.**
- **Keep calcium away from beef meals by about 2 h.** In practice: the shake sits at breakfast (no beef), the beef goes at lunch and dinner, and haldi doodh stays at 21:00. The one conflict is a cheese or curd side with beef, so drop the curd bowl from beef meals.
- **Vitamin C with beef:** kiwi, guava or lemon. **This already matches his "beef pairs with kiwi/fruit" preference.**
- **Tea or coffee at least 1 h away from meals** (tannins).
- **Avoid a training-day energy deficit.** Low energy availability raises hepcidin, which blocks iron absorption ([athlete iron review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/332877238_Iron_considerations_for_the_athlete_a_narrative_review)). Dates or a banana before Cult are part of the iron plan, not a cheat.
- Athlete iron-stores cut-off: **ferritin < 30 µg/L = low stores** ([Clénin et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26512429/)).
