> **Streak restarted. Day 1 = tomorrow.**
> Your life = the content. 5-10 sec clips throughout the day. Edit at night. Post next morning.
> B-roll + text overlay style. Minimal talking. Minimal phone time.

---

## THE CONCEPT

One reel = one day compressed into 20-30 seconds.

**Format:** 5-8 clips of 2-4 sec each → text overlay on each → trending audio → done.

**Why this works for you:**
- No talking to camera (less self-conscious, less phone time)
- Clips are 5-10 sec each — phone is out for seconds, not minutes
- You're already doing everything — zero extra work to create content
- Seeing your full day compiled = instant motivation
- Documentary style = authentic, not performative
- **The Day counter is your strongest hook.** Day 1, 2, 3... people come back for the streak.

## HOW TO CAPTURE (Phone Out = Seconds, Not Minutes)

### The 10-Second Rule:
```
1. Phone OUT of pocket/stand
2. Hit record
3. 5-10 sec of footage
4. Stop recording
5. Phone AWAY (pocket, face down, shelf)
```

**Total phone-out time per day: under 3 minutes across 5-8 clips.**

### Pro tips:
- **Prop the phone** against a book, mug, shelf — don't hold it. Frees your hands.
- **Top-down shots** for food/desk: hold phone flat over the surface, 3 sec.
- **Walk-by shots**: prop phone on a shelf, walk past it. Looks cinematic.
- **Screen recordings**: use built-in screen recorder (NOT Instagram). 3-5 sec of your IDE/LeetCode.
- **Don't narrate.** The text overlay + audio tells the story. Silence is fine.
- **Morning clips are gold** — "04:45 AM" as text = instant hook. Nobody's awake = mystique.
- **MMA clips are your most engaging content** — action shots, sweat, intensity. Get at least 1 every training day.

## EDITING (CapCut Desktop, 15-20 min at Night)

### Flow:
```
1. Transfer all clips to laptop (cable/Drive)
2. Open CapCut desktop
3. Import clips in ORDER (chronological = your day)
4. Trim each clip to 2-4 sec (cut all dead space)
5. Add text overlay on each clip
6. Add ONE trending audio track (low volume, 15-20%)
7. Add "Day X of 84" text at the start
8. Export 1080p, 30fps
9. Total length: 20-30 sec
```

### Text overlay style:
- **Font:** Bold, clean, white with black outline
- **Position:** Center or lower third
- **Duration:** Match the clip (2-4 sec each)
- **Keep it short:** "04:45 AM" not "I woke up at 4:45 AM today because..."
- **Day counter:** Always start with "Day 1" → "Day 2" → "Day 3" — the streak IS the content

### Audio:
- Pick trending audio from CapCut's library (search "motivation" or "discipline" or "lofi")
- Keep volume LOW (15-20%) — it's background, not the main thing
- Check Instagram Reels tab for trending audio (↗ arrow icon) → use those

## PHONE BOUNDARY (CRITICAL — READ THIS)

This format is the SAFEST for your relapse chain because phone-out time is seconds, not minutes. BUT:

```
✅ DO:
  □ Shoot 5-10 sec → phone AWAY immediately
  □ Edit on LAPTOP (CapCut desktop, not phone)
  □ Post from LAPTOP browser (Instagram web)
  □ Check analytics ONCE per day from laptop

❌ DON'T:
  □ Don't watch the clip back on your phone — wait until laptop
  □ Don't "check Instagram" after posting — post from web, close tab
  □ Don't scroll "for inspiration" — your life IS the inspiration
  ⚠️ If you feel the urge to "just check" → that's the old pattern. Phone face down.
```

**The trap to watch for:** "Let me just review the clips" on your phone → 45 min gone → no sleep → miss Golden Block. **Transfer to laptop FIRST, review there.**

1. **Separate Instagram account** — not your personal one
2. **No prayer/salah/wudu footage ever** — keep deen sacred
3. **No TCS client data, project names, or internal tools** on screen
4. **Instagram app STAYS BLOCKED on personal phone** — post from laptop browser ONLY

6. **Reels = TOOL, not reward** — don't let "making content" become the new scroll
7. **20 min max per phone session** — timer on, phone face down when done
8. **Golden Block (04:45-06:15) = NO reel work. Ever.** Film in 5 sec, move on.
9. **Spring Boot block (21:15-22:15) = NO reel work. Ever.** Film in 5 sec, move on.
10. **Bar first, reel after** — hit your DSA + career bar, THEN film/edit

## WHAT NOT TO FILM
- ❌ Prayer / salah / wudu footage (keep sacred)
- ❌ Office screens with sensitive data (TCS client info, project names)
- ❌ Other people at gym/office without consent
- ❌ Anything that feels performative — you're documenting, not performing
- ❌ Debt numbers, salary details, or personal financial info on screen

## RED FLAGS (Stop Immediately If...)
- You're scrolling Instagram "for inspiration" > 10 min → you're scrolling, not creating
- You're checking views more than 2x/day → phone goes in another room
- Reels eating into study time → pause reels for a week, no guilt
- You feel the urge to "just check" → that's the old pattern wearing a new mask
- You're spending more time editing than studying → reel quality doesn't matter yet
