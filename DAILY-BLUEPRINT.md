1. Resume salah at the next prayer (office Dhuhr/Asr + post-Isha family call anchor)

Miss during Recovery Mode → another 3 days. Nothing is erased. 7+ consecutive misses = honest review alert, never a blank `paused` state. **There is no V5.**
