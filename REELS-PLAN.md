## Phone Boundary Protocol (quick ref — full version in REELS-GUIDE.md)

```
SHOOTING:
  □ Set 30-min timer on phone
  □ Shoot clips
  □ Timer off → STOP
  □ Phone face down, out of reach
  □ Transfer clips to laptop immediately

EDITING:
  □ CapCut DESKTOP only (not phone)
  □ 45 min max, timer on

POSTING:
  □ From laptop browser (Instagram web)
  □ App stays blocked on phone
  □ Post, reply to comments for 15 min, CLOSE tab

ANALYTICS:
  □ Check ONCE per day (not every hour)
  □ From laptop only
  □ Don't let numbers become the new dopamine
```
