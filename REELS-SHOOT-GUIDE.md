## PHONE SETUP (Do Once, 2 min)

```
1. Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p / 30fps
2. Settings → Display → Auto-lock → Never (while shooting)
3. Clean the lens. Seriously. Wipe with your shirt.
4. Turn ON grid lines (Settings → Camera → Grid)
5. Do Not Disturb ON — no notifications mid-shoot
6. Put phone on something eye-level (stack of books, shelf, tripod)
   - DON'T hold it — you need your hands for props/gestures
7. Face a window. Light on your FACE, not behind you.
```

## BATCH RECORDING WORKFLOW (Shoot 5 Reels in 30 Min)

This is the key: **don't record one reel per day. Record 5 at once.**

### Why batch?
- One setup (lighting, framing, energy) → 5 outputs
- Phone is out for 30 min, not 2 hours spread across the week
- You're "on" once, not 5 separate times
- Edit later on laptop at your own pace

### Before You Hit Record (5 min setup):
```
□ Pick 5 topics from the content calendar
□ Write each one as 3-5 bullet points on PAPER (not phone)
□ Set up phone at eye level, facing window
□ Do a 5-sec test clip → watch it → check lighting + audio
□ Have water nearby (dry mouth = bad takes)
```

### The 30-Min Session:
```
REEL 1 (6 min):
  □ Hit record
  □ Deliver hook with ENERGY (first 3 sec)
  □ Deliver 3-5 bullet points naturally
  □ Deliver CTA ("Follow if you want to see how it goes")
  □ Stop recording
  □ Watch it back ONCE
  □ If terrible → 1 retake. If okay → move on. Don't perfect.

REEL 2 (6 min): Same flow, different topic
REEL 3 (6 min): Same flow, different topic
REEL 4 (6 min): Same flow, different topic
REEL 5 (6 min): Same flow, different topic

DONE. Phone face down. Transfer clips to laptop.
```

### After Batch Session:
```
□ AirDrop / cable / Google Drive → transfer clips to laptop
□ Phone goes AWAY (face down, out of arm's reach)
□ Edit on CapCut desktop later (Sun afternoon batch edit)
□ Post throughout the week from laptop browser
```

## WHAT TO RECORD — Shot List (Pick From These)

### Type 1: Talking Head (You + Camera)
**Setup:** Phone on stand, eye level, 2-3 feet away, window behind phone

| Reel Idea | Hook (Say This First) | Bullet Points | CTA |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|-----|
| Why I'm switching | "Everyone says you need a CS degree for tech. You don't." | 1. I'm non-tech, switching to SWE 2. 12-week plan 3. No bootcamp, just discipline | "Follow to see if it works" |
| My daily routine | "I wake up at 4:15 AM. Not because I'm a morning person." | 1. Tahajjud 2. Golden Block DSA 3. MMA 7 AM 4. Office 5. Spring Boot at night | "Follow for the journey" |
| 3 things I learned | "3 things I wish I knew before starting DSA prep." | 1. It's patterns not IQ 2. Consistency > intensity 3. Re-solve to retain | "Save this for later" |
| Honest day 1 | "Day 1 of my career switch. Here's what actually happened." | 1. Woke up late 2. Solved 1 problem 3. Prayed 3/5 4. Missed Spring Boot | "Follow for the real version, not the highlight reel" |
| Why MMA + coding | "I train MMA and learn coding. Both need the same thing." | 1. Show up every day 2. Reps beat theory 3. Lose before you win | "Follow if you're grinding too" |

**Delivery tips:**
- Talk like you're explaining to a friend, not teaching a class
- Use your hands — stiff arms = stiff delivery
- Smile slightly on the hook — warmth reads on camera
- Pause 1 sec between points (easier to edit later)
- If you mess up — STOP, pause 2 sec, restart from the last point. You'll cut the bad part in editing.

### Type 2: Screen Recording (Code Walkthrough)
**Setup:** Laptop screen recording + phone mic (or laptop mic)

```
1. Open IntelliJ / LeetCode on laptop
2. Screen record (OBS, or built-in: Win+G on Windows, Cmd+Shift+5 on Mac)
3. Talk through your solution line by line
4. Keep it to 30 sec — show the KEY insight, not the whole code
5. Hook example: "Watch me solve Two Sum in 30 seconds."
```

### Type 3: B-roll (No Talking, Text Tells Story)
**Setup:** Phone propped somewhere, recording you doing things

```
WHAT TO CAPTURE (30-sec clips each):
  □ Morning: making your shake / pre-workout fuel
  □ Walking to gym / MMA training (gloves, bag, mat)
  □ Opening laptop, IntelliJ on screen
  □ Journal + pen on desk
  □ Walking commute
  □ Cooking (batch meal prep)

EDITING:
  □ 2-3 sec per clip (fast cuts)
  □ Text overlay: "Day 1 of 84" / "Career switch journey"
  □ Trending audio (low volume)
  □ No talking needed
```

## BATCH RECORDING TIPS

1. **Wear the same outfit** — if you batch 5 reels, they look like different days if shirt is same
2. **Change angle slightly** between reels — move the phone 6 inches left/right so they look different
3. **Energy drops by reel 3** — that's normal. Take 60 sec, drink water, reset.
4. **Don't re-record more than once** — "good enough" posted > "perfect" never posted
5. **Label clips immediately** — after transferring, rename: `reel01-why-switch.mp4`, `reel02-routine.mp4` etc.
6. **Shoot B-roll separately** — during the week, grab 5-10 sec clips of MMA, cooking, commute. Store in a folder. Use them as filler/transition footage.
