## 4. The new bar system (v4.0 — 3 tiers)

This is the biggest change. Your day is now scored like this:

| Tier | What it takes | Effect |
|------|---------------|--------|
| 🟩 **FLOOR** | Fajr + camp (or declared rest) + current salah rung | **Streak stays alive.** Day does NOT increment. |
| 🔵 **DAY** | Floor + one worked item (1 DSA / 30-min Spring Boot / design / mock) | **Day +1** |
| 🟡 **STRONG** | Floor + 2+ DSA + a Spring Boot commit | Day +1, noted |
| 😴 **REST** | You say "rest day" | Streak preserved. Zero guilt. |
| ⬜ **MISSED** | Floor not met | Streak resets. One sentence. Move on. |

**What this means on a bad day:** pray Fajr, go to camp, do your salah rung — and the streak SURVIVES. You can have a rough week and the system never tells you you're worthless. Career output is what mints a Day; deen and training are what keep you alive.

**Sep 4–13 reclassified:** 9 × 🟩 floor days, 0 career days. Honest on both counts.

## 7. Recovery Mode (replaces all future "restarts")

Triggers automatically the morning after any ONE of: phone past cutoff delaying sleep · re-sleep after Fajr · post-camp binge · binge-caused missed day.

**Runs 3 calendar days, same arc, nothing erased:**
1. Resume salah at the next prayer (office Dhuhr/Asr + post-Isha family call anchor)
2. Fajr → camp → airlock → office. No bed/sofa after camp.
3. One preselected 10–25 min career task, phone physically away
4. Reels: filming fine, publishing queued only
5. No tahajjud target, no extra MMA, no catch-up, no "restart tomorrow"
6. Phone away after family call, sleep 21:30

Miss during Recovery Mode → another 3 days. **A slip is a 3-day protocol now, not a new arc.** There is no V5.
