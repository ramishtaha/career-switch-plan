## GUARDRAILS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **Separate Instagram account** — not your personal one, no link, no friend DMs
2. **No prayer/salah/wudu footage EVER** — keep deen sacred. Riyaa eats reward. *"Whoever prays to be seen, Allah will expose him."* (Bukhari)
3. **Documentary/journey style** — not performative, not trends for trends' sake
4. **Instagram app STAYS BLOCKED on personal phone** — post from laptop browser ONLY
5. **After 5-6 days → move to second device** for filming
6. **Reels = TOOL, not reward** — don't let "making content" become the new scroll
7. **20 min max per phone session** — timer on, phone face down when done
8. **Golden Block (04:45-06:15) = NO reel work. Ever.** Film in 5 sec, move on.
9. **Spring Boot block (21:15-22:15) = NO reel work. Ever.** Film in 5 sec, move on.
10. **Bar first, reel after** — hit your DSA + career bar, THEN film/edit

> ⚠️ **Break a guardrail = no reel that day.** The habit system > the documentation.

## THE CONCEPT

One reel = one day compressed into 20-30 seconds.

**Format:** 5-8 clips of 2-4 sec each → text overlay on each → trending audio → done.

**Why this works for ADHD:**
- No talking to camera (less self-conscious, less phone time)
- Clips are 5-10 sec each — phone out for seconds, not minutes
- You're already doing everything — zero extra work
- Seeing your full day compiled = instant motivation
- Documentary style = authentic, not performative
- **The Day counter is your strongest hook.** Day 1, 2, 3... people come back for the streak

## PHONE SETUP (Do Once, 2 min)

```
1. Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p / 30fps
2. Settings → Display → Auto-lock → Never (while shooting)
3. Clean the lens. Wipe with shirt.
4. Turn ON grid lines (Settings → Camera → Grid)
5. Do Not Disturb ON — no notifications mid-shoot
6. Put phone on something eye-level (stack of books, shelf, tripod)
   - DON'T hold it — you need your hands
7. Face a window. Light on FACE, not behind.
```

## THE 10-SECOND RULE

```
1. Phone OUT of pocket/stand
2. Hit record
3. 5-10 sec of footage
4. Stop recording
5. Phone AWAY (pocket, face down, shelf)
```

**Total phone-out time per day: under 3 minutes across 5-8 clips.**

### Pro tips:
- **Prop the phone** against a book, mug, shelf — don't hold it
- **Top-down shots** for food/desk: hold phone flat over surface, 3 sec
- **Walk-by shots**: prop phone on shelf, walk past. Cinematic.
- **Screen recordings**: built-in screen recorder (NOT Instagram). 3-5 sec.
- **Don't narrate.** Text overlay + audio tells the story.
- **Morning clips are gold** — "04:45 AM" = instant hook. Nobody's awake = mystique.
- **MMA clips are most engaging** — action, sweat, intensity. Get 1 every training day.

## EDITING (CapCut Desktop, 15-20 min at Night)

```
1. Transfer all clips to laptop (cable/Drive)
2. Open CapCut desktop
3. Import clips in ORDER (chronological = your day)
4. Trim each clip to 2-4 sec (cut all dead space)
5. Add text overlay on each clip
6. Add ONE trending audio track (low volume, 15-20%)
7. Add "Day X of 84" text at the start
8. Export 1080p, 30fps
9. Total length: 20-30 sec
```

### Text overlay style:
- **Font:** Bold, clean, white with black outline
- **Position:** Center or lower third
- **Duration:** Match the clip (2-4 sec)
- **Keep short:** "04:45 AM" not "I woke up at 4:45 AM today because..."
- **Day counter:** Always start with "Day 1" → "Day 2" → streak IS the content

### Audio:
- Pick trending audio from CapCut library (search "motivation" / "discipline" / "lofi")
- Volume LOW (15-20%) — background, not main
- Check Instagram Reels tab for trending audio (↗ arrow) → use those
